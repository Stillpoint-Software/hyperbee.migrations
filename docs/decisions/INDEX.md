| 0029 | [The Ledger's Wire Contract is Library-Owned](0029-ledger-wire-contract-is-library-owned.md) | Accepted | 2026-08-25 | Which index/collection a ledger document lives in, and what its fields are named on the wire, is library-owned -- never inherited from consumer-configured client inference.
